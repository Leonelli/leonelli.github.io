<section class="block" id="biography">
  <p class="eyebrow">Biography</p>
  <p>
    Matteo Leonelli is a Ph.D. fellow at the CISPA Helmholtz Center for Information Security,
    supervised by Prof.&nbsp;Dr. Thorsten Holz. Prior to this, he earned an M.Eng. in Security from
    Saarland University (2024) and a B.Sc. in Computer Science from the University of Trento (2021).
    His research focuses on fuzzing, automated testing, and vulnerability discovery, with a
    particular emphasis on novel approaches for detecting unusual behaviors and bug classes.
  </p>
</section>
